# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][covenant], a
community standard for inclusive open-source projects.

The full text — including Our Pledge, Our Standards, Enforcement
Responsibilities, Scope, Enforcement, Enforcement Guidelines, and
Attribution — is available at the link above and is incorporated here by
reference. Maintainers commit to following it; contributors agree to
abide by its terms when participating in this project's spaces (issues,
pull requests, discussions, and any official channels).

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may
be reported to the maintainers responsible for enforcement at
**conduct@athanor.dev**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly. All maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy
and security of the reporter of any incident.

[covenant]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
